# Mango Turmeric Chia Seed Pudding

A quick, lightly spiced chia pudding with mango and turmeric. Great for breakfast, a snack, or meal prep.

Ingredients

* 1 tbsp chia seeds
* 60g plain unsweetened greek yogurt
* 1/4 cup milk of choice
* 60g  fresh or frozen mango
* 1/4 tsp ground turmeric
* Pinch of ground ginger, optional
* Pinch of black pepper, optional
* Honey or maple syrup, to taste, optional

Instructions

1. Cut the mango into cubes and set aside
2. Pour the milk into a small jar or container.
3. Stir in the chia seeds, turmeric, and any optional spices or sweetener.
4. Let sit for about 5 minutes, then stir again to prevent the chia seeds from clumping.
5. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, preferably overnight.
6. Add greek yogurt layer on top of chia seed mixture once formed. 
7. Add Mango layer on top of Greek Yogurt. 
 
Dig in and enjoy!

Notes

* Frozen mango works well and makes this easy to prepare year-round, but fresh mango is a divine treat when available!
* Adjust the amount of milk slightly depending on your preferred consistency.
* Top with fresh mango, coconut, granola, or nuts before serving.
