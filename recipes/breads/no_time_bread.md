# No-Time Bread

A quick homemade yeast bread for when I want fresh bread without waiting on a long rise.

Adapted from *How To Make No-Time Bread* by The Kitchn.

## Ingredients

- 2 packets active dry yeast (4 1/2 tsp)
- 1 tbsp granulated sugar
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 3 1/2 cups bread flour
- 1 1/4 tsp salt
- 3/4 tsp balsamic vinegar
- Small amount of neutral oil for the bowl

## Instructions

1. Combine the yeast, sugar, and water and let the yeast begin to activate.

2. Preheat the oven to **450°F** with a Dutch oven inside.

3. Add 3 cups of the flour, the salt, and balsamic vinegar to the yeast mixture.

4. Mix, then add the remaining 1/2 cup flour. Knead until the dough becomes elastic. The dough should remain somewhat wet and tacky.

5. Lightly oil a large microwave-safe bowl and place the dough inside.

6. Cover with a very wet kitchen towel, then a dry towel.

7. Microwave on high for **25 seconds**.

8. Let the dough rest in the microwave for **5 minutes**.

9. Microwave for another **25 seconds**, then let it rest for another **15 minutes**.

10. Shape the dough into a ball.

11. Carefully transfer it into the hot Dutch oven and slash the top.

12. Cover and bake for **30 minutes**.

13. Remove the lid and bake for roughly **10 more minutes**, until deeply golden and the internal temperature reaches about **210°F**.

14. Cool before slicing.

## Notes

- This is my quick bread option when I don't have time for a long fermentation.
- The balsamic vinegar adds a little tang that mimics some of the flavor of a longer rise.
- Best baked in a preheated Dutch oven for a crisp crust.
- Leftovers can be stored at room temperature for a few days or frozen.
