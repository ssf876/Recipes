# Jamaican Coco Bread 🇯🇲

A soft, slightly sweet Jamaican bread with a rich, buttery texture. A bakery and patty-shop staple, and the perfect companion for a Jamaican beef patty.

## Ingredients

- 4 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 1/4 tsp active dry yeast
- 2 tbsp granulated sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 cup warm coconut milk
- 1/2 cup warm water
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- 1 large egg
- Additional melted butter, for brushing

## Instructions

1. Combine the warm water, yeast, and a little of the sugar. Let sit for 5–10 minutes, until foamy.

2. In a large bowl, combine the flour, remaining sugar, and salt.

3. Add the warm coconut milk, melted butter, egg, and activated yeast mixture.

4. Mix until a shaggy dough forms, then knead until the dough is smooth and elastic, about 8–10 minutes by hand.

5. Place the dough in a lightly greased bowl, cover, and allow it to rise until approximately doubled in size, about 1–1 1/2 hours.

6. Punch down the dough and divide it into equal portions.

7. Roll each portion into an oval, brush lightly with melted butter, and fold it in half to create the traditional coco bread shape.

8. Arrange the breads on a parchment-lined baking sheet. Cover and allow them to rise again for about 30–45 minutes.

9. Preheat the oven to **350°F**.

10. Brush the tops with additional melted butter.

11. Bake for approximately **15–20 minutes**, until lightly golden.

12. Brush with a little more melted butter while still warm.

13. Allow to cool slightly before serving.

## Serving Ideas

- Stuff with a Jamaican beef patty
- Serve alongside jerk chicken
- Use for breakfast sandwiches
- Eat warm with butter
- Use as a soft sandwich bread

## Notes

- Coco bread gets its name from **coconut**, not cocoa.
- The dough should remain soft. Avoid adding too much additional flour while kneading.
- Brushing the dough with butter before folding helps create the characteristic seam and rich interior.
- Coconut milk contributes richness and subtle coconut flavor.
- Best served warm and fresh, but freezes well.

## Origin

**Jamaica 🇯🇲**

Coco bread is a popular Jamaican bakery bread, especially well known for being paired with a Jamaican beef patty.
