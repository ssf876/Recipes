# Ground Turkey Pad Kra Pao

...
Ground Turkey Pad Kra Pao

My ground turkey adaptation of Pad Kra Pao, with molasses for a little extra depth and optional spinach for more greens.

Adapted from The Woks of Life’s Pork & Holy Basil Stir-Fry (Pad Kra Pao).

Ingredients

* 1 lb ground turkey
* 3 tbsp neutral cooking oil
* 2 shallots, thinly sliced
* 7 cloves garlic, sliced or minced
* 3 Thai bird chilies, thinly sliced, or to taste
* 1 tsp molasses
* 1 tsp sugar
* 1 tbsp fish sauce
* 1 tbsp light soy sauce
* 2 tsp dark soy sauce
* 2 tsp oyster sauce
* 1/3 cup low-sodium chicken broth or water
* 1 1/2 cups packed holy basil leaves
* 2–3 handfuls fresh spinach, optional

For Serving

* Jasmine rice
* Fried egg, optional

Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a wok or large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add the shallots and garlic and cook for about 3 minutes, stirring frequently.
3. Add the chilies and cook for another minute.
4. Increase the heat to high. Add the ground turkey and break it into small pieces as it cooks. Let the turkey develop some browning rather than constantly stirring it.
5. Add the molasses, sugar, fish sauce, light soy sauce, dark soy sauce, and oyster sauce. Stir well so the turkey is evenly coated.
6. Add the chicken broth or water to deglaze the pan. Continue cooking over high heat until most of the liquid has reduced.
7. If using spinach, add it and stir-fry until just wilted.
8. Add the holy basil last and toss until the leaves are wilted and incorporated into the turkey.
9. Serve immediately over jasmine rice, optionally topped with a fried egg.

Notes

* I substitute ground turkey for the traditional ground pork.
* I add 1 tsp molasses per pound of ground turkey for additional depth and sweetness.
* Spinach isn’t traditional, but I often add it for extra greens.
* Holy basil is ideal, but Thai basil or regular basil can work when holy basil isn’t available.
* Adjust the chilies depending on your preferred heat level.

Source

Adapted from Pork & Holy Basil Stir-Fry (Pad Kra Pao) by The Woks of Life.
