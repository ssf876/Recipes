Three Bean Chili

A hearty beef chili with black beans, kidney beans, and baked beans, finished with your favorite toppings.

Ingredients

* 2 tablespoons olive oil
* 1 medium yellow onion, diced
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 3 tablespoons tomato paste
* 2 pounds ground beef
* 2 teaspoons kosher salt
* 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
* 2 tablespoons chili powder
* 1 tablespoon ground cumin
* 1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder
* 2 teaspoons dried oregano
* 1 teaspoon paprika
* 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper, optional
* 1 can black beans, drained and rinsed
* 1 can kidney beans, drained and rinsed
* 1 can baked beans
* 1 28-ounce can crushed tomatoes, preferably fire-roasted
* 1 14.5-ounce can low-sodium beef or chicken broth
* 1 teaspoon apple cider vinegar

For serving

* Sour cream
* Sliced green onions
* Shredded cheese, optional

Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until softened.
2. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant.
3. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for a few minutes, until it darkens slightly.
4. Add the ground beef, salt, and black pepper. Cook, breaking the beef apart as it browns, until fully cooked.
5. Add the chili powder, cumin, cocoa powder, oregano, paprika, and cayenne. Stir well and cook briefly until fragrant.
6. Add the black beans, kidney beans, baked beans, crushed tomatoes, and broth. Stir to combine and bring to a boil.
7. Reduce the heat and simmer uncovered until the chili has thickened to your liking, stirring occasionally.
8. Remove from the heat and stir in the apple cider vinegar. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.
9. Serve topped with sour cream and green onions. Add shredded cheese if you want it.

Notes

* Adapted from The Kitchn’s Classic Beef Chili.
* The baked beans add a slightly sweeter flavor than a traditional beef chili.
* Chili keeps well in the refrigerator and is especially good the next day.
