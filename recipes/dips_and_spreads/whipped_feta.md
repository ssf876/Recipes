Whipped Feta

A creamy, savory whipped feta with garlic, herbs, and a little heat. Courtesy of my friend Chanelle and her incredible taste.

Ingredients

* 7 oz high-quality feta cheese
* 6 tbsp Greek yogurt
* 1 garlic clove, minced
* 2 tbsp olive oil
* 1 1/2 tsp herbes de Provence
* Pinch of chili flakes

Instructions

1. Add the feta and Greek yogurt to the bowl of a food processor fitted with the blade attachment.
2. Add the olive oil and minced garlic.
3. Add the herbes de Provence and chili flakes. Pulse on low speed for 10–20 seconds.
4. Scrape down the sides of the bowl as needed.
5. Pulse again for about 20 seconds, or until the whipped feta reaches your desired smoothness.
